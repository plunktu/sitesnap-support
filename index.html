Snap the job. Talk for seconds. Send the quote.

Bidsmith is a quote and invoice app for solo contractors, handymen, detailers, and cleaners on iPhone and iPad.

  1. Snap before and after photos of the job.
  2. Talk through the work in a short voice memo.
  3. Send a branded PDF with itemized labor and materials, tax, a before/after photo grid, and your client's signature.

Built for the job site

  • Works offline: quotes and PDFs are made on your device.
  • No account and no tracking. Your quotes stay on your phone.
  • Clear pricing, a reminder before any free trial ends, and real support.
