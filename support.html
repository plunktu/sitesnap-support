Frequently asked questions

How does SiteSnap Quote work?

Add 2 to 4 before/after photos, record a short voice memo describing the job, and SiteSnap turns it into itemized labor and materials lines with tax. Review the lines, get the client's signature on screen, and tap Create PDF & Share to send a branded PDF quote or invoice.

Can I try it without talking?

Yes. On the New Quote tab, tap Load 5-Second Sample Voice Quote. On the plans screen, Try Sample Voice Dictation builds a free sample PDF marked SAMPLE.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Quotes, photos, and PDFs are made on your phone. With no connection, SiteSnap uses its built-in parser; type or use the keyboard's dictation mic for the job description.

Where are my quotes stored? Is there an account?

There is no account. Everything is saved on your device. Your iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud or computer) can include it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change my business name, logo, rate, or tax?

Open the Settings tab. Changes apply to new PDFs.

How does the free trial work, and will I be reminded?

The plans screen shows your exact trial length, the date you'll be billed, and the price, before you confirm. If you allow notifications, we remind you before the trial ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

I cancelled, but I was still charged

Subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends, so a cancellation made in the last day can still renew once. Check your subscription status and, if a charge looks wrong, request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles all refunds; we're happy to help, just email us.

I have a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open SiteSnap, and tap Restore Purchases on the plans screen (Settings tab > See plans).

What happens to my Quote Credits if I reinstall?

Credits from a Quote Credit pack are stored on the device you bought them on. Apple can't restore one-time packs, so they are lost if you delete the app or switch devices. Credits never expire while the app stays installed.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, including split view. Apple Pencil works great for client signatures.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you manage them in your Apple account:

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap SiteSnap Quote.
  4. Change plans, or tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends.

From inside SiteSnap

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Manage Subscription. This opens the same Apple screen.

Or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. After cancelling, you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases

On the plans screen (shown after setup, or Settings tab > See plans), tap Restore Purchases. You must be signed in with the Apple ID that bought the subscription.

Reset or delete your data

Before you reset: share or save any PDFs you need (for example to Files). Resetting can't be undone, and we have no copy to recover.
  1. Touch and hold the SiteSnap Quote icon on the Home Screen.
  2. Tap Remove App, then Delete App.
  3. This deletes every quote, client, photo, signature, PDF, your business profile, and any Quote Credits from this device.
  4. Reinstall from the App Store to start fresh. Tap Restore Purchases to bring back an active subscription.

If your device backs up to iCloud, an older backup can still contain SiteSnap data. To remove it, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage > Backups, choose the device, and turn off SiteSnap Quote. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription; see above.

To delete the anonymous purchase records held by our purchase-validation provider, email us.
