5. Quote Credit packs

  • A Quote Credit pack is a one-time, non-renewing (consumable) purchase. Each pack adds the number of credits shown at purchase (currently 25). Each new quote or invoice PDF uses one credit.
  • Credits do not expire and have no cash value. They are stored on the device where you bought them. Apple does not restore consumable purchases, so credits are not restored by Restore Purchases and are lost if you delete the app or move to a new device.
  • Re-sharing a quote you already saved does not use a credit.
